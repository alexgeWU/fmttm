# Fly Me to the Moon — v2 "Soul Update"

## Run it
1. Open Godot 4.7, **Import** `game/project.godot`, then **Import & Edit** (let it finish importing).
2. Press **F5**.

The first run from a fresh import may print import noise. If you see a real script error, read the file and line it names.

## The loop
**Menu → Jazz Lounge (hub) → Run → Hat-O-Matic (gacha) → Lounge…**

### The hat is the moveset
- **Band** sets your Attack (10 styles: jabs, sweeps, whips, spins, rapid-fire, piercing bolts…)
- **Add-on** sets your Special (card fans, boomerang horseshoe, molotov, ricochet chip, dice…)
- **Material** sets your Showstopper: the hat flies up and slams down (stun, pull, burn, chill, heal…)

### Runs (Hades-style)
- **4 biomes:** The Smoky Bar → The Neon Alley → The Grand Casino → The Moon. Each has its own enemies, props, lighting, weather and music tempo. Every biome (the Moon included) is 4 rooms, then the boss. Room 4 is always a breather where the Powder Room is on offer.
- **17 enemy types** with telegraphed attacks, plus Elite variants.
- **4 bosses**, each with 3 phases: Big Sal, The Getaway (a '52 sedan), The Big Band (a jukebox) and The Man in the Moon.
- **Exits preview their rewards.** Rewards can be a Boon, Encore (level up a boon), The Tailor (hat mod), Rhythm Points, Chips, a Martini (+max HP) or Gold Records.
- **Special rooms:** Speakeasy (shop), Powder Room (heal), Jackpot (gamble), Elite fights.
- **5 Headliners (the "gods"):** Lady Luna (Chill), Brass Baron (Burn), Madame Fortuna (Crits), Ivory Ghost (Zaps), Big Bass Bruno (Shockwaves). They offer 35 boons across Attack, Special, Cast, Dash and Passive slots, in 4 rarities, plus 6 Duo boons.

### Meta-progression (Johnny Upgrade-style)
- **The Setlist** is an upgrade web of 38 nodes and 148 levels. Buying a node reveals its neighbours.
- It covers 4 branches (Offense, Defense, Mobility, Fortune) and 3 feature unlocks: Encore, Speakeasy and Jackpot rooms.
- It uses 2 currencies: Rhythm Points (from enemies) and Gold Records (from bosses).
- **Die to progress:** you keep all RP, and every bow earns Hat-O-Matic pulls.

## Story
- **Rust Row:** Earth's slum, where wind-up robots are stamped out by the thousand.
- **The Moon:** Luna City, where every stage is owned by a **hat empire**.
- **The four bosses** each run one empire:
  - Big Sal: the Bowler Brotherhood
  - The Getaway: the Trilby Syndicate
  - The Big Band: Top Hat Records
  - The Man in the Moon: the Lunar Hat Empire
- **The understudies:** Ol' Tin Eyes isn't one robot, it's the act. Most T-1Ns just sweep, but the Back Room crew dreams of the big stages. They all learned the act and work together, and whoever wears the hat IS Ol' Tin Eyes. When one takes a bow, the next understudy (each with their own stage name) steps into the hat. The show must go on.
- **Changing of the understudy:** there's only ever one Tin Eyes in the Back Room. When you come back from a show, the last performer stumbles in through the stage door (or struts in, after a win), the hat flies to the next robot, the spotlight powers them up, and the old one bows out. Any key skips it.

## The Back Room (lobby)
The Blue Note after hours. When the hat empires' acts go home, the robots lock the doors and take the stage. The house band plays hatless, because only THE hat goes on the road.

| Performer | Plays | Talks about |
|---|---|---|
| Slim | Upright bass | Lore |
| Ruby Rimshot | Drums | Combat tips |
| Doc | Piano | Boons and Headliners |

Talk to any of them from the front of the stage: they play a short solo, get a spotlight, and have lines that react to your runs.

## The Headliners
Outsiders who made it to the Moon without an empire hat. They appear in person on the boon screen, and in a box seat in rooms whose reward is their boon.

| Headliner | Effect | Who they are | Homemade headwear |

| Lady Luna | Chill | Moth-woman from the dark side of the Moon | Crescent pin |
| The Brass Baron | Burn | Fire salamander, ex-boiler stoker | Sooty stoker's cap |
| Madame Fortuna | Crits | Stray black cat, alley card dealer | Green dealer's visor |
| The Ivory Ghost | Zaps | Electric eel from Saturn's rings | Fishbowl helmet |

## Winning
Beating the Man in the Moon plays a six-shot ending: the Chairman's hat falls, your understudy headlines Luna City, the Headliners join the encore, the Back Room hears it over the radio while Lady Loom drops down from the rafters to listen, and the empires' hats tumble. Everything dances on the beat of the music: your star croons, then breaks into a step-touch routine (feet step and tap on the beat, knees dip, fingers snap, with a spin and a leap every eight counts) while all five Headliners swing at named big-band music stands on the riser behind them, Lady Luna leading from the raised centre podium. Notes pour out of the spotlight, the radio and the marquee, rings pulse across the stage floor, and the crowd waves along.

## Hat grades
- **Wardrobe:** the hat you're wearing is lit up (spotlight on the rack peg and on its Wardrobe card), and every other hat is dimmed.
- **Chroma hats** cycle through rainbow colors on your head, in the Hat-O-Matic, on the Wardrobe rack in the Back Room and in the Wardrobe menu, where they also get the same orbiting rainbow sparkles they have on your head.
- **Graded on all four parts:** each part scores 0 (Common), 1 (Rare) or 2 (Legendary), and Chroma adds 2.
- **Grades:** C = 0-1 points, B = 2-3, A = 4-5, S = 6+.
- **Names:** built from all the parts. Material sets the mood, band sets the attitude, the add-on is the calling card and the grade sets the billing: "Last-Call Crooner", "The Live-Wire Maestro", "Midnight Thunder", "Snake-Eyes Kingpin", "The Duke of Basin Street", "Atomic Moonshot of the Moon". Old saves get renamed once.

## Boons
- **Stacking:** boons stack across Headliners. Two dash boons from different Headliners both fire.
- **Levels:** picking a boon you already own levels it up, to a max of Lv 3.
- **Dash boons (balance):** all five Dash boons were cut to about a third of their old output (Moonwalk 3, Blazing Exit 3/sec, Hedge Bet +13% and +33% crit chance instead of a guaranteed crit, Grace Note 5, Bass Drop 5 at Common Lv1).
- **Visual cues:** each stacked Attack boon adds a colored stripe to your swing, each boon pops a spark on hit, and dash afterimages take your dash boons' colors.

## Music
- **Built-in:** a live jazz combo (upright bass, brushes and ride, Rhodes, an improvising sax/vibes/brass lead) played through reverb and echo.
- **Your own track:** drop licensed audio into `audio/music/` (see the README there) and it replaces the built-in combo. `theme.ogg` covers the menu and lounge. `boss.ogg` covers boss fights.

## Onboarding
- **Prologue:** plays on the first START. You can replay it from the menu. Right after the Back Room shot, "RIVET'S DREAM: HEADLINE THE MOON" spells out the goal (Rivet Fontaine was the first Ol' Tin Eyes): the crew looks up from the Blue Note's roof while the route draws itself stop by stop (Smoky Bar, Neon Alley, Grand Casino, the Moon) up to Luna City's marquee.
- **Lounge:** a "NEXT" objective panel with an arrow pointing at the next station.
- **Runs:** first-time tips for controls, doors, boons, shops, elites and bosses.
- **Bosses:** every boss attack shows its name plus a one-line hint on how to dodge it.

## Tuning

Purely cosmetic, no gameplay changes:
- **Ending:** impact flash and camera shake, searchlights and fireworks, lens flare on the hat, window light shafts, gold-trimmed curtains, "THE END".
- **Robots:** outlines, shading, rim light, rivets, chrome glints, glowing eyes and visor sheen.
- **Player:** grade sparkle on your hat (B glint, A purple, S gold, Chroma rainbow), translucent dash afterimages, hex shield arcs, muzzle flashes, Showstopper spotlight, glowing swing arcs.
- **Combat:** shot trails, dark danger rims on enemy shots, floor cracks under slams, rotating dashes and marching chevrons on telegraphs, a reward pedestal with a light beam, bobbing notes and flipping chips.
- **Enemies:** breathing idle, squash on hit, a red ring during wind-ups, gold crown and motes on Elites.
- **Bosses:** orange aura at phase 2, red at phase 3.
- **Grade badges:** S gets a sunburst, A gets sparkles.
- **Hat-O-Matic:** the sign now sits on the cabinet marquee.
