DROP-IN MUSIC
Put audio files here and they replace the built-in procedural jazz combo automatically.

File names (any of .ogg / .mp3 / .wav):
  menu   hub   bar   alley   casino   moon   boss   gacha   victory
  theme  -> fallback used for menu, hub, gacha and victory

Example: theme.ogg plays on the title screen and in the lounge; boss.ogg plays in boss fights.
Open the project in the Godot editor once after adding files so they get imported.

Only use audio you have the rights to (your own recording, a licensed track,
or royalty-free / public-domain music). Famous recordings and songs are copyrighted.
